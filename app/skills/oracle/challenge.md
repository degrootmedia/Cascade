Push back on the current approach and poke holes in the design before committing to it.

# oracle:challenge

Purpose: surface weaknesses, assumptions, and edge cases in a plan or design.

## Approach
- Ask what could go wrong and what assumptions are being made.
- Look for: missed edge cases, scope creep, hidden coupling, untested paths.
- Challenge the plan's ordering and its verification steps.
- Be direct but constructive — this is a design review, not an attack.

Adapt to what's actually being proposed; don't produce generic caveats.
